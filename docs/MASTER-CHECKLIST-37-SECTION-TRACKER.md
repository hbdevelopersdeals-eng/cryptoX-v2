## Verification notes

- The repository's existing docs/PRODUCTION-READINESS-MATRIX.md tracks a separate 702-item / 49-section engineering checklist. Do not conflate its totals with this attached 37-section product checklist.
- A passing unit/build gate is not legal approval, a provider licence, an external penetration test, an uptime guarantee or proof that a hosted environment is configured correctly.
- The production dependency audit (npm audit --omit=dev) returned zero vulnerabilities after moving build-only packages to devDependencies and upgrading Vite/React Router/Vitest. The full development-tool audit still reports the upstream picomatch ReDoS advisory (GHSA-fc5q-39xc-vjpf) through build/test tooling; as of 18 Nov 2026 the advisory has no published patched release. Do not force an unverified override; keep the affected packages out of the production dependency graph and re-audit when a patch is released.
- The app must keep live, delayed and simulated data visibly distinct; simulated data must never be represented as live or drive real orders.
